# Photo Assets Checklist

Gather these assets to replace placeholder visuals and strengthen credibility across the site.

## Priority 1: Operations

- Warehouse interior (shelving, inventory, packing stations)
- Order fulfillment or shipping area
- Suggested use: About page, Market Entry page, future homepage operations section

## Priority 2: Company presence

- Pittsboro office exterior or team photo
- Suggested use: About page, Contact page (supports LocalBusiness trust)

## Priority 3: Brand lifestyle photography

- Sierra Socks product or lifestyle imagery
- Wear Sierra marketplace or product imagery
- Suggested use: Our Brands section on homepage (replace logo-only hero blocks)

## File guidelines

- Format: JPG or PNG, minimum 1600px wide for hero use
- Place files in `src/assets/images/` once received
- Name examples: `warehouse.jpg`, `office.jpg`, `sierra-socks-lifestyle.jpg`, `wear-sierra-lifestyle.jpg`

After upload, wire each image into the relevant content module in `src/content/`.
